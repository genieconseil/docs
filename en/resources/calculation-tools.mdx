# Calculation Tools & Software for Engineers

Choosing the right software can make the difference between a productive engineering workflow and hours of manual work. This guide covers the key tools by discipline, with notes on cost, accessibility, and student licensing.

<Info>
  The full French version of this page with additional Canadian context is available at [/ressources/outils-calcul](/ressources/outils-calcul). The French page [/ressources/logiciels-par-discipline](/ressources/logiciels-par-discipline) provides a comprehensive discipline-by-discipline software matrix.
</Info>

## Structural & Civil Engineering

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="ETABS / SAP2000 / SAFE" icon="building">
    **CSI (Computers & Structures Inc.)** — Industry standard for building and structural analysis. ETABS for buildings, SAP2000 for general structures, SAFE for slabs/foundations. Student licenses available via university programs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="RISA-3D / RISA Floor" icon="grid-2">
    **RISA Technologies** — Popular in North America for steel, concrete, and wood design. Integrates with Revit. Widely used by consulting firms.
  </Card>
  <Card title="STAAD.Pro" icon="diagram-project">
    **Bentley Systems** — Common in industrial and infrastructure projects. Strong pipe stress and offshore modules.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Revit Structure" icon="cube">
    **Autodesk** — BIM authoring for structural models. Free student/educator license via Autodesk Education Community.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Free / Open Source Structural Tools
- **OpenSees** — Open-source finite element platform for earthquake engineering (UC Berkeley). Free.
- **SkyCiv** — Cloud-based structural analysis, free tier available for simple beams and trusses.
- **ClearCalcs** — Spreadsheet-style structural calculations, free tier for basic members.
- **StructX** — Free online structural calculators (beams, columns, connections).

| Software | Developer | Key Use | License |
|----------|-----------|---------|---------|
| GeoStudio (SLOPE/W, SEEP/W, SIGMA/W) | Seequent/Bentley | Slope stability, seepage, stress-deformation | Commercial |
| Plaxis 2D/3D | Bentley | Advanced FEM geotechnical | Commercial |
| RS2 / RS3 | Rocscience | Rock mechanics, tunnels, slopes | Commercial |
| SETTLE3 | Rocscience | Settlement analysis | Commercial |
| FLAC / FLAC3D | Itasca | Continuum geomechanics | Commercial |

  Rocscience offers a **free 30-day trial** and educational licenses for students. GeoStudio student licenses are available through Bentley's academic program.
</Tip>

## Hydraulics & Hydrology

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="HEC-RAS" icon="droplet">
    **US Army Corps of Engineers** — The standard for river hydraulics, flood modelling, and dam break analysis. **Free** to download. Version 6+ includes 2D modelling capabilities.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HEC-HMS" icon="cloud-rain">
    **US Army Corps of Engineers** — Hydrological modelling (rainfall-runoff, unit hydrographs, routing). **Free**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SWMM" icon="city">
    **US EPA** — Storm Water Management Model for urban drainage and sewer systems. **Free**. InfoSWMM and PCSWMM are commercial frontends.
  </Card>
  <Card title="InfoWorks ICM" icon="waves">
    **Autodesk** — Integrated catchment modelling combining 1D/2D surface and sewer network simulation. Industry standard for complex urban drainage.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Mechanical & Thermal Engineering

| Software | Primary Use | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| SolidWorks | 3D CAD, design, FEA (Simulation) | CSWA/CSWP certifications; student license via SOLIDWORKS Education |
| ANSYS Mechanical | FEA, structural, thermal | ANSYS Student (free, limited size); used in most engineering schools |
| Abaqus/CAE | Non-linear FEA, crash, composites | Dassault; academic license available |
| COMSOL Multiphysics | Multiphysics coupling | Very powerful for coupled thermal-structural-fluid problems |
| Autodesk Inventor | 3D CAD, simulation | Free via Autodesk Education |
| Fusion 360 | CAD/CAM/CAE | Free for students and startups (revenue <$1M/year) |

## Electrical Engineering

| Software | Use | License |
|----------|-----|---------|

| MATLAB + Simulink | Control systems, signal processing, power electronics | Student license ~$50/year; free via many universities |

## Chemical & Process Engineering

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Aspen HYSYS / Aspen Plus" icon="flask">
    **AspenTech** — The industry standard for process simulation. HYSYS for oil & gas; Aspen Plus for chemical and refining. Student/university licenses available through AspenTech Academic Program.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DWSIM" icon="flask">
    **Open source** — Free, Python-based process simulator. Covers thermodynamics (Peng-Robinson, NRTL, etc.) and unit operations. Excellent for learning and small projects.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ProMax" icon="industry">
    **Bryan Research & Engineering** — Specialized for gas treating, LNG, and sulfur recovery. Common in Western Canada gas processing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CHEMCAD" icon="atom">
    **Chemstations** — Full process simulation suite. More affordable alternative to Aspen for smaller firms.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Mining Engineering

- **Leapfrog Geo** (Seequent/Bentley) — 3D geological modelling and resource estimation. Industry standard.
- **GEOVIA Surpac** (Dassault) — Mine design and geological database management.
- **Vulcan** (Maptek) — Block model creation, open pit and underground design.
- **Deswik.Suite** — Underground mine design and scheduling.
- **Whittle** (Dassault) — Pit optimization using Lerchs-Grossmann algorithm.
- **MineSight** (Hexagon) — Ore body modelling and mine planning.

## General Calculation & Math Tools

| Tool | Best For | Cost |
|------|----------|------|
| **MATLAB** | Engineering computation, signal processing, control systems | Commercial (~$150/student/year or free via university) |
| **Python** (NumPy, SciPy, Pandas, Matplotlib) | Data analysis, scientific computing, automation | **Free** |
| **Mathcad** (PTC) | Engineering calculation documentation with live math | Commercial; free trial available |
| **Maple** (Maplesoft — Canadian!) | Symbolic math, differential equations | Commercial; educational licenses |
| **Excel** + engineering add-ins | Quick calculations, data tables, unit conversions | Included in Microsoft 365 |
| **Wolfram Alpha** | Quick formula lookups, unit conversions | Free (basic), Pro subscription available |

## Project Management Software

- **Microsoft Project** — Scheduling, resource leveling, critical path. Free via Microsoft 365 Education.
- **Oracle Primavera P6** — Enterprise-grade for large projects. Common in EPC and construction.
- **OpenProject** — Open-source alternative. Self-hosted, free.
- **Procore** / **Autodesk Construction Cloud** — Construction management platforms.

## AI Tools for Engineering

<Warning>
  AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot) can help with code generation, documentation, and formula lookup, but **must never replace engineering judgment**. Always verify AI-generated calculations independently. Never stamp or seal work that hasn't been fully reviewed by a licensed professional.
</Warning>

**Productive AI use cases for engineers:**
- Generating Python/MATLAB scripts for data processing
- Writing and formatting technical reports
- Searching for applicable standards and code clauses
- Explaining unfamiliar engineering concepts
- Code review and debugging for simulation scripts
- Generating first drafts of specifications (requires expert review)
