# Engineering Project Management

Effective project management is a core competency for engineers. Whether managing a $500K building renovation or a $5B infrastructure megaproject, the fundamentals of scope, schedule, cost, and risk apply universally.

<Info>
  This page is the English version. The full French content with Canadian-specific references is available at [/gestion-projets/planification](/gestion-projets/planification).
</Info>

## Engineering Project Lifecycle

Engineering projects follow a phased approach that gates decisions and controls risk:

| Phase | Also Known As | Key Deliverables | Cost Estimate Accuracy |
|-------|---------------|------------------|----------------------|
| Conceptual | Pre-feasibility, Screening | Project scope, order-of-magnitude cost | ±30–50% (AACE Class 5) |
| Pre-FEED | Feasibility, Scoping | Basis of design, preliminary layout | ±20–30% (AACE Class 4) |
| FEED | Preliminary Engineering | Process flow diagrams, equipment lists | ±10–20% (AACE Class 3) |
| Detailed Engineering | Final Design | Construction drawings, specs, BOQ | ±5–10% (AACE Class 2) |
| Procurement & Construction | Execution | Built asset | ±5% (AACE Class 1) |
| Commissioning & Startup | Handover | Tested, operational facility | — |

<Tip>
  Gate reviews (Go/No-Go decisions) at the end of each phase prevent investing detailed engineering dollars in projects that won't meet business objectives. Use AACE Class 5 estimates ruthlessly at early gates.
</Tip>

## Scheduling Techniques

### Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
Decompose the project scope into manageable work packages — typically to a level where one person or team can own the work, and duration can be estimated with confidence.

### Critical Path Method (CPM)
The critical path is the longest sequence of dependent activities — any delay on the critical path delays the project.

**Forward Pass** (Early Start / Early Finish):
- `ES = max(EF of all predecessors)`
- `EF = ES + Duration`

**Backward Pass** (Late Start / Late Finish):
- `LF = min(LS of all successors)`
- `LS = LF - Duration`

**Total Float** = `LF - EF` = `LS - ES` (zero on critical path)

### PERT (Program Evaluation and Review Technique)
When activity durations are uncertain, use three-point estimates:

`Expected Duration (TE) = (Optimistic + 4 × Most Likely + Pessimistic) / 6`

`Standard Deviation (σ) = (Pessimistic - Optimistic) / 6`

### S-Curve Tracking
Plot cumulative planned value (PV) vs. actual cost (AC) and earned value (EV) over time. An S-curve flattening early signals schedule slippage.

## Earned Value Management (EVM)

EVM integrates scope, schedule, and cost into a unified performance measurement system:

| Metric | Formula | Meaning |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Schedule Variance (SV)** | EV − PV | Negative = behind schedule |
| **Cost Variance (CV)** | EV − AC | Negative = over budget |
| **Schedule Performance Index (SPI)** | EV / PV | <1.0 = behind schedule |
| **Cost Performance Index (CPI)** | EV / AC | <1.0 = over budget |
| **Estimate at Completion (EAC)** | BAC / CPI | Forecasted total cost |
| **Variance at Completion (VAC)** | BAC − EAC | Projected over/under budget |

<Warning>
  A CPI below 0.85 in the first 20% of a project is a strong predictor of final cost overrun. Early corrective action is far more effective than late recovery.
</Warning>

## Risk Management

### Risk Register
Document all identified risks with:
- **Description** — what could happen and why
- **Probability** — likelihood (1–5 or %)
- **Impact** — consequence on cost/schedule/quality (1–5)
- **Risk Score** — Probability × Impact
- **Risk Owner** — accountable person
- **Response Strategy** — avoid, transfer, mitigate, accept

### Risk Response Strategies

| Strategy | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **Avoid** | Eliminate the threat | Change scope to avoid contaminated soil |
| **Transfer** | Shift risk to third party | Insurance, fixed-price contract |
| **Mitigate** | Reduce probability or impact | Add geotechnical investigations early |
| **Accept** | Acknowledge and monitor | Small-probability, low-impact risks |

### Monte Carlo Schedule Risk Analysis
Use tools like Oracle Primavera Risk Analysis or @RISK (Palisade) to run thousands of schedule simulations with probabilistic durations. Output a P50 (most likely) and P80 (80% confidence) completion date.

## Procurement & Contracting

### Contract Types

| Contract Type | Risk Bearer | Best For |
|---------------|-------------|---------|
| Lump Sum / Fixed Price | Contractor | Well-defined scope, low uncertainty |
| Cost-Plus (T&M) | Owner | Poorly defined scope, R&D projects |
| Unit Price | Shared | Civil works with variable quantities |
| Design-Build (DB) | Contractor | Fast-track, single point of responsibility |
| P3 (DBFMO) | Private consortium | Large infrastructure, long lifecycle |

### Canadian Public Procurement
- **Federal**: Public Services and Procurement Canada (PSPC), MERX platform, AMP/CETA thresholds
- **Quebec**: Act Respecting Contracting by Public Bodies (Bill 12), SEAO platform, lowest compliant bidder rule (some exceptions for quality weighting)
- **Ontario**: Infrastructure Ontario, Supply Ontario, BPS Procurement Directive
