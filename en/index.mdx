Welcome to **GénieConseil**, a bilingual engineering knowledge base designed for practicing engineers, engineering students, and technical professionals. This English section covers key topics across civil, mechanical, electrical, and software engineering disciplines.

GénieConseil's mission is to provide practical, formula-driven reference content — the kind of knowledge you reach for on the job: key formulas, design guidelines, code references, and tooling recommendations — presented clearly and concisely.

<Note>This site is primarily written in French. The English section provides comprehensive coverage of the most important topics. Visit the French sections for additional depth and detail on topics such as reinforced concrete design, fluid mechanics, renewable energy, control systems, and risk management.</Note>
